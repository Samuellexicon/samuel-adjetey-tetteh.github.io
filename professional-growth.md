### Project 2: Host-Based Intrusion Detection & Subnet Reconnaissance Lab (*Wazuh SIEM & Nmap*)
* **Context & Task:** Deployed a centralized security monitoring environment using a Wazuh SIEM manager connected to both Ubuntu Server and Windows Server agents, alongside active network reconnaissance using Nmap across a `/20` subnet in Kali Linux.
* **Challenges & Solutions:** Initial agent-to-manager communication failed due to firewall port restrictions and certificate mismatches, while broad subnet scans generated noisy output that obscured critical vulnerabilities. I resolved connectivity by auditing stateful packet filters, verifying agent registration keys, and refining Nmap scan flags to isolate open ports and service versions accurately.
* **Results & Lessons Learned (Technical & Spiritual):** Successfully captured real-time file integrity alerts, authentication anomalies, and vulnerability baselines across cross-platform VMs. This project reinforced my principle of **Singleness of Focus** (3 Nephi 13:22). In cybersecurity, overlooking small misconfigurations creates blind spots; similarly, keeping an "eye single" to Christ requires removing distractions so I can act with clarity, honesty, and vigilance in protecting organizational data.

### Project 3: Relational Database Engineering & Operational Analytics (*JFK Flight Delays & Chinook Media Store*)
* **Context & Task:** Engineered complex SQL queries in PostgreSQL using JetBrains DataGrip to analyze operational datasets. For the JFK Airport dataset, I joined weather and flight tables to measure correlations between precipitation and departure delays. For the Chinook database, I used window functions and Common Table Expressions (CTEs) to build an automated multi-disc compilation algorithm.
* **Challenges & Solutions:** Raw datasets contained schema mismatches, null values, and time-zone discrepancies that skewed initial delay averages. I cleaned and normalized the data types, structured modular CTEs for readability, and validated edge cases against expected business logic.
* **Results & Lessons Learned (Technical & Spiritual):** Produced accurate, reproducible analytical queries and translated complex metrics into clear, jargon-free insights for non-technical stakeholders. Working through frustrating data anomalies strengthened my commitment to **Christ-Centered Peace and Resilience Under Pressure**. Relying on prayer, patience, and methodical troubleshooting allowed me to stay calm when queries failed, proving that spiritual peace directly improves analytical precision and teamwork.
