
# Spiritual Progression Portfolio

## My Guiding Principles

My spiritual and professional development are connected. I want my faith, education, decisions, and professional responsibilities to reflect the values I am working to develop.

### 1. Christ-Centered Integrity and Service

I will strive to keep Jesus Christ at the center of my decisions, relationships, education, and professional responsibilities. Integrity means being honest, accepting responsibility, respecting others, and doing what is right even when nobody is watching.

In my professional life, this principle means protecting confidential information, following safety procedures, being accountable for my work, and treating users and colleagues with respect. Whether I am working in industrial maintenance or Information Technology, I want people to be able to trust me.

### 2. Purposeful Learning and Stewardship

I believe my education, time, opportunities, and abilities are responsibilities that should be used wisely. I will pursue continuous learning with discipline, humility, and a clear purpose.

My transition from industrial electrical maintenance into Information Technology requires commitment and consistent effort. As I develop my knowledge of Linux, Windows Server, cloud infrastructure, networking, and cybersecurity, I want to use what I learn to solve problems and serve others.

### 3. Resilience, Unity, and Continuous Improvement

I will remain committed to growth through challenges while valuing kindness, cooperation, and unity. My industrial maintenance experience has taught me to investigate problems carefully, identify root causes, and work toward reliable solutions.

These lessons also apply to Information Technology. Reliable systems require teamwork, careful planning, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement. I want to communicate clearly, support others, and build resilient systems while developing resilience in my own life.

## My Spiritual and Professional Direction

These principles will guide my decisions as I continue my education and develop my career in Systems Administration, Cloud Infrastructure, and Cybersecurity. My aim is to grow spiritually and professionally without allowing one area of my life to replace the other.
