
# Initial Materials and Resources

These resources will guide my study and reflection as I develop my three guiding principles.

## Scripture Study

1. **3 Nephi 13:19–22 — Treasures in Heaven and the Single Eye**
   - Principle: Purposeful Learning and Stewardship.
   - Lesson: Consider what I value most and direct my attention toward God.
   - [Read the scripture](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/13?lang=eng&id=19-22#p19)

2. **Mosiah 2:17 — Service to Others**
   - Principle: Christ-Centered Integrity and Service.
   - Lesson: Serving others is also serving God.

3. **Doctrine and Covenants 6:36 — Looking to the Savior**
   - Principle: Resilience, Unity, and Continuous Improvement.
   - Lesson: Look to Christ in every thought and face challenges with faith.

## Video Study

4. **Jesus Christ: The Prince of Peace**
   - Principle: Christ-Centered Integrity and Service.
   - Study focus: Identify how the Savior's example can influence my decisions, relationships, and response to challenges.
   - [Watch the video](https://youtu.be/5QHClYjiCF8)

5. **Blessings of the Temple**
   - Principle: Christ-Centered Integrity and Service; Resilience and Unity.
   - Lesson: Kindness, unity, love, respect, and the blessings of temple ordinances for families and ancestors.
   - [Watch the video](https://youtu.be/3e8mbYvspqo)

6. **Don't Take the Temple for Granted**
   - Principle: Purposeful Learning and Stewardship.
   - Lesson: Do not allow convenience or distractions to weaken commitment to spiritual priorities.
   - [Watch the video](https://youtu.be/zaD1FGeEsM4)

## General Conference Study

7. **David A. Bednar — Gather Together in One All Things in Christ**
   - Principle: Resilience, Unity, and Continuous Improvement.
   - Study focus: Consider how spiritual and professional responsibilities can be brought into harmony through Jesus Christ.
   - [Read the talk](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2018/10/gather-together-in-one-all-things-in-christ?lang=eng)
