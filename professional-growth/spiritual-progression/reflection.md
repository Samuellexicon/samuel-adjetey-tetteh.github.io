# My Reflection Activity

## Reflection Experience

**Date of reflection:** November 13, 2026

**Location:** Quiet study room at home (Tema, Ghana)

**Activity:** I stepped away from my computer and daily tasks for some quiet, uninterrupted time to pray, read 3 Nephi 13:19–22 and Doctrine and Covenants 88:118, and reflect on my covenants alongside Elder David A. Bednar’s analogy of intertwined strands.

## What I Learned

Taking time to slow down brought a real sense of gratitude and peace. Reading 3 Nephi 13:19–22 reminded me how easy it is to let daily pressures pull my attention in ten different directions—and how much clearer life becomes when I keep my "eye single" to God. Thinking about Elder Bednar’s image of intertwined strands also shifted my perspective. For a long time, it felt like work, school, family, and church responsibilities were competing for the same limited hours. Seeing them as strands woven together around Jesus Christ helped me realize they actually reinforce one another when my priorities are in the right order.

## Connection to My Professional Growth

This reflection helped me see the common thread running through my past career and where I am headed next. Over the past ten years working in industrial electrical maintenance, I learned that keeping critical systems running takes patience, careful diagnostics, and steady accountability. Those same habits apply directly to my transition into IT systems administration and cloud infrastructure—and they mirror the steady effort it takes to honor covenants every day. When a server goes down or a security alert fires, panic never solves the problem. Having a foundation of Christ-centered peace helps me stay calm, think clearly, and support my team with integrity.

## My Commitments Going Forward

* **Spiritual Commitment:** Guard dedicated daily time for scripture study and prayer, and be deliberate about temple worship so busy schedules never cause me to take sacred covenants for granted.
* **Academic & Technical Commitment:** Carry the principle of learning "by study and also by faith" into my current and upcoming IT courses—building hands-on skills in Linux, cloud infrastructure, and security while documenting my work clearly.
* **Workplace & Leadership Commitment:** Bring patience, honesty, and a calm, service-oriented mindset into every project, team interaction, and future Systems Administrator role.
